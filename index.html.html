How is your salary calculated?

  1. Gross Salary is derived from the CTC after accounting for employer PF (CTC = Gross + Employer PF).
  2. Basic = 50% of Gross.
  3. HRA = 20% of Gross.
  4. Conveyance = 15% of Gross.
  5. Special Allowance = 15% of Gross.
  6. PF Wage = Gross − HRA = 80% of Gross.
  7. Employee PF = 12% of PF Wage, capped at ₹3,000 when PF Wage reaches ₹25,000.
  8. Employer PF follows the same calculation.
  9. Employee ESIC = 0.75% of ESIC Wage when ESIC Wage is below ₹21,000.
  10. Net In-Hand = Gross − Employee PF − Employee ESIC.

Solving CTC → Gross without circularity: if CTC < ₹34,250, Gross = CTC ÷ 1.096 (Employer PF = 9.6% of Gross); otherwise Gross = CTC − ₹3,000.
